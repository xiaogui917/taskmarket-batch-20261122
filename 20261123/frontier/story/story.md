# The Day the Realm Moved

You wake before the fog lifts. The map is new — it is new every morning — and your realm stands in the middle of it, walls and barracks and stores all where you left them. The land around it is not. The land is a question, and you have one army to ask it with.

Your barracks trained the troops through the night. You deploy them beside your realm, and they step out as one army, torches lit, into the grey. The Scout walks ahead, lantern low. What sits on a tile is decided the moment your army reveals it, so nobody can scout ahead — not you, not anyone. The first tile gives up a well, and the army drinks, and strength comes back to tired legs. The second gives up Essence, and it goes straight home to your realm, as every reveal does.

By noon the fog shows you a shrine on a low hill, and the army kneels a moment for the skill point it holds. Then the ground changes. A troll stands guard over a broken camp, and you see the guard and what it pays before you order the attack. No dice are rolled here. The forecast is the result, and the forecast says the camp falls. It falls.

The haul goes home — everything the reveal and the clear paid, straight to your realm. You can feel the realm grow heavier in the good way: another course of stone on the wall, another tier of the castle taking shape, another line of research inked beside the spire, where the Ethereal depths wait for a day you are not yet ready to open.

Dusk comes down like a door closing. The map, every site, every army — gone. You stand on the wall and watch the fog take it all back, and you are not afraid, because you know the rule now, the way you know your own name: tomorrow the land is gone, and your realm is not. It will rise again on the next map with its buildings, its research, its troops and its stores, and you will send another army into another morning.

The Scout hangs the lantern by the gate. The realm sleeps. It has grown a little, and it will grow again.

**Teaser:** One army, one day of fog, and a realm that refuses to stay behind.
