Frontier meme pack — notes

1. Angle: 9 — Your realm grows: buildings, castle tiers and research that carry over.
2. Tools and models: PIL (Pillow) image compositing with Python 3; copy written by Muse (Muse Spark). No image-generation model was used; all art comes from the Frontier brand kit. Exo 2 ships in the kit's converted fonts as Regular 400 only, so the meme type is Exo 2 with a ground-coloured outline plus a 1px multi-draw to stand in for Exo 2 Bold.
3. Non-kit audio/fonts/images: none / kit only. Plates (plate-army-into-fog-portrait, plate-empty-fog-square, plate-spire-depths-landscape), Scout poses (character-scout-comic.png), frontier-lockup.png, Exo 2 — all from the Frontier brand kit.
4. Existing entries: I reviewed the submission list (not the entry files themselves — the SSH channel to the submission store was down, so no entry files could be opened): 14 entries, mostly named meme1/2/3.png; NovaShiftRunner's three are titled chest-or-beast, the-scout-who-cannot-scout and you-just-did.
